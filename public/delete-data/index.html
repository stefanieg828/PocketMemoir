delete your data

pocket memoir keeps your scrap album on your device — not on our servers. this page is the public “delete data” url for play console and anyone who wants to clear their scraps.

last updated: september 28, 2026

how to delete

  • on the web / pwa — clear this site’s browsing data (cookies & site data / local storage) for pocketmemoir.fun, or remove the installed shortcut / pwa.
  • on android — clear the app’s storage & cache in system settings, or uninstall pocket memoir. that removes local scraps and unlock flags on that device.
  • off-device / help request — email scraps@pocketmemoir.fun with a subject like “delete my data”. we do not host your scrap albums, but we can confirm what (if anything) processors may still hold and point you to the right controls.

what is deleted locally

clearing site/app data or uninstalling removes what pocket memoir stored on that device, including:

  • your scraps (notes, photos you added, stickers, shelf layout)
  • unlock flags and related preferences kept in localStorage

we do not host user scrap albums on a pocket memoir server, so there is no cloud scrapbook for us to wipe after you clear the device.

backups or peek files you previously downloaded or shared only exist where you put them — delete those copies yourself if you no longer want them around.

what may remain briefly elsewhere

  • stripe (optional web unlock) — payment / receipt records may remain with stripe under their retention rules. we do not store your full card details.
  • google play billing (android / play store) — purchase records may remain with google play under google’s terms.
  • optional google analytics — if analytics is enabled via a measurement id, google may retain soft usage signals for a short period under its own policies. scrap content is not sent in those events.

those processors keep their own records; pocket memoir cannot erase stripe or play purchase history from this page. email us if you need help figuring out the next step.
