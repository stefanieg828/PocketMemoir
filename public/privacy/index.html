privacy policy

pocket memoir is a private scrapbook for your notes, photos, tickets, lists, and little wins. this page explains what stays on your device, what you choose to share, and the few optional services that may run when you use the app.

last updated: september 28, 2026

no account required

you do not need to create an account to use pocket memoir. there is no signup, no login, and no profile we keep for you on our servers.

scraps stay on your device

your scraps (notes, photos you add, stickers, shelf layout, unlock state, and related preferences) are stored locally in your browser or app using localStorage on this device. we do not host your scrapbook content on pocket memoir servers.

if you clear site data, uninstall the app, or switch devices without a backup, those local scraps may be gone — so keep a backup when it matters.

backups & peeks you choose to share

  • backup — you can save or share a backup file so you can move your scraps between devices. that file only goes where you send it (download, share sheet, email, files app, and so on).
  • peek — you can share a read-only peek file so a friend can browse your album without changing theirs. again, the file only travels through channels you pick.

pocket memoir does not upload backups or peeks for you. if you share a file with someone else, their copy is under their care — share only what you are comfortable letting them see.

optional unlock (payments)

  • on the web — unlocking “big scraps” may use an optional stripe payment link. stripe processes the payment; we do not store your full card details. stripe’s own privacy policy applies to that checkout.
  • on android — google play billing is planned for the play store build. when that ships, google’s billing and privacy terms will cover the purchase flow.

unlock state can also travel inside a backup you restore. a peek never grants unlock.

optional google analytics

if the site is built with a VITE_GA_MEASUREMENT_ID set, pocket memoir may load google analytics 4 for soft usage signals such as page views and light engagement events (for example unlock landing, tour finish / skip, or dismissing an add-to-home tip).

  • scrap text, photos, emails, and other scrap content are not sent in analytics events.
  • scrap ids in paths are redacted (for example /kept/:id).
  • when that measurement id is unset, analytics stays off and gtag does not load.

google may process analytics data under its own terms. you can also use browser or device controls that limit tracking.

cookies & similar tech

the core scrapbook does not depend on advertising cookies. local storage holds your album. if google analytics is enabled, google may set or read its own cookies / identifiers as described in google’s documentation.

children

pocket memoir is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

changes

we may update this policy as the app grows (for example when play billing lands). the “last updated” date above will change when we do. continued use after an update means you are okay with the revised note.
