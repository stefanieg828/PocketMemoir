New kinds (add to person / place / thing)
ENTRY_KINDS: person | place | thing | moment | event
Moment — a scene or day, not a scheduled thing.
titleHint: “the storm on Oak” / “first snow”
howLabel: “When / what kind”
howHint: “winter 2019, a power outage”
factsHint: “who was there, what we ate”
note: one line
optional date (past, can be fuzzy: year-only ok if you already store a timestamp; prefer a date field)
Event — dated, past or future. Feeds the calendar.
titleHint: “Sam’s thing” / “move-out” / “the concert”
howLabel: “What kind”
howHint: “birthday, trip, appointment I care about”
factsHint: “who, where, what to remember”
date required (day precision). Future allowed.
optional link-by-title to a person/place later; not required in v1.5
Do not add Recipe, Hobby, Pet, or Journal page in this pass.
Filters: All · People · Places · Things · Moments · Events
Calendar
A month grid. Not a planner that nags.
Dots on days that have an Event (and Moments that have a real date)
Tap a day → list those cards + Keep this day (creates Event or Moment with that date)
Future dates work
No “you missed Tuesday,” no streaks, no daily prompt
Month label + prev/next
Home can keep the shelf. Calendar is its own tab/page: Shelf | Calendar | search as now.

date on Event (required), optional on Moment / Person / Place / Thing
second photo not required this pass
wouldBuyAgain stays on Thing only
